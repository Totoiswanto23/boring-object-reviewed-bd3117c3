## The entry

> The doorstop, one star: it has held the same door open since 2019, and its only thanks is a kick from everyone who walks through.

129 characters, one line. The joke is built on a true, recognisable detail: a doorstop does one thankless job for years, and the only attention it ever gets is being kicked out of the way by the very people it serves. It dodges the obvious objects (rubber band, paperclip) and stays specific to the doorstop — only a doorstop gets kicked for doing its job perfectly.
